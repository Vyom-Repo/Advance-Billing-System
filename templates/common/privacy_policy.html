1 Introduction

Advance Billing ("Platform", "Service", "we", "us", or "our") provides cloud-based software-as-a-service (SaaS) invoicing, client management, product cataloging, tax computation, and billing automation services designed for Indian sole proprietors, freelancers, small businesses, and enterprise organizations.

Users utilize Advance Billing to configure business profiles, manage customer/vendor directories, store product catalogs with HSN/SAC classifications, generate GST-compliant PDF invoices, dispatch transactional emails, manage payment preferences, and configure automated backups.

This Privacy Policy comprehensively explains how we handle information processed across our Platform. By accessing or using Advance Billing, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2 Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all services operated by Advance Billing, including our public landing pages, authenticated web application dashboard, document rendering API engines, email dispatch facilities, automated weekly backup services, and public health endpoints.

This policy does not apply to third-party services, external websites, or infrastructure platforms that we do not directly own or control, each of which operates under its own privacy terms.

Advance Billing is intended for use by individuals and businesses operating within the Republic of India. By creating an account, you represent that you are accessing the Service from India for the purpose of conducting business subject to Indian law. This Policy is drafted with reference to Indian data protection law and does not separately address obligations arising under other jurisdictions' data protection frameworks (e.g., the EU GDPR or US state privacy laws).

3 Legal Definitions

For the purposes of this Privacy Policy, terms are defined in alignment with applicable Indian laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, and the Information Technology Act, 2000:

  • "Digital Personal Data" / "Personal Data": Any data about an individual who is identifiable by or in relation to such data, rendered in digital form.
  • "Data Principal": The individual to whom the personal data relates (e.g., registered account holder or end customer).
  • "Data Fiduciary": Any person or entity who alone or in conjunction with others determines the purpose and means of processing personal data.
  • "Data Processor": Any person or entity who processes personal data on behalf of a Data Fiduciary.
  • "Business Data": Non-personal or commercial information entered by users, including organization names, GSTINs, bank account details, line items, and transaction totals.
  • "Personal Data Breach": Any unauthorized processing, disclosure, alteration, destruction, or loss of personal data that compromises its confidentiality or integrity.

4 Information We Collect

We process information submitted by users or generated through Platform usage across six categories:

A. Account & Registration Information

When creating an account, we collect user full names, email addresses, password hashes (cryptographically secured via PBKDF2/Argon2), account status, and organization team memberships.

B. Business & Organization Information

Users enter organization legal names, trade names, business addresses, state, GSTIN, PAN identifiers, business emails, phone numbers, bank account numbers, IFSC codes, bank names, UPI IDs, invoice numbering prefixes, and custom terms.

C. Customer & Vendor Directory Data

Users may store client or supplier details including customer names, business names, billing addresses, shipping addresses, phone numbers, contact emails, and GSTINs.

D. Product & Service Catalog Data

Catalog information entered by users includes item names, descriptions, HSN/SAC codes, default unit prices, measurement units, tax rates (CGST, SGST, IGST), and discount settings.

E. Invoice & Transaction Data

Invoices generated on the Platform contain invoice numbers, issue dates, due dates, buyer/seller details, line item descriptions, quantities, rates, sub-totals, CGST/SGST/IGST tax splits, total values, payment statuses, and notes.

F. Uploaded Assets

Users may upload business branding assets such as company logos, authorized signature images, or letterhead graphics. Users are responsible for ensuring they possess the right to upload such materials.

5 Technical and Usage Information

When interacting with the Platform, our application servers automatically log technical metadata, including IP addresses, browser user-agents, operating system types, request timestamps, HTTP request paths, status codes, rate-limit counters, and error tracebacks.

Technical data is processed exclusively for server security, abuse prevention, rate-limit enforcement, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, and incident investigation.

6 Service Health Monitoring

Advance Billing operates a public health endpoint at /health/ consumed by cloud zero-downtime deployment monitors (such as Render) and external uptime trackers (such as UptimeRobot). This endpoint returns non-personal application health metrics (status, application version, environment). It does not process or expose personal data or system credentials.

7 How We Use Information

We process information strictly for lawful, operational purposes:

  • Rendering the SaaS dashboard, invoice creation forms, and directory interfaces.
  • Automating GST tax calculations (CGST, SGST, IGST) based on State of Origin and Place of Supply rules configured by the user.
  • Generating formatted PDF invoice documents in real-time.
  • Dispatching requested invoice emails and transactional notifications to designated recipients.
  • Generating and emailing automated weekly data backups (JSON + Excel) to organization owners when enabled.
  • Enforcing multi-tenant database security, user authentication, and API rate limiting.
  • Complying with statutory audit trail requirements under applicable Indian law.

8 Purpose Limitation, Legal Basis, and Consent

Processing of personal data is undertaken for specified, explicit, and lawful purposes under the DPDP Act, 2023. We process user account data based on contract performance (providing requested services), compliance with legal obligations, or explicit user consent where applicable.

Where consent is the basis for processing, it is captured through a clear affirmative action at the point of account registration (acceptance of this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service). Consent is specific to the purposes described in this Policy and may be withdrawn at any time via the account settings page or by writing to our Grievance Officer. Withdrawal of consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal, and may result in certain Platform features becoming unavailable.

9 Information You Choose to Provide

Users control the information entered into Advance Billing. Users are responsible for ensuring that all business details, customer information, tax parameters, and uploaded files are accurate, lawful, non-infringing, and provided in compliance with applicable laws.

10 Customer and Vendor Data

When users input personal data of their end-customers, vendors, or business contacts into Advance Billing, the user is responsible for determining their legal authority to collect and process such information, including providing required privacy notices or obtaining consent under the DPDP Act. Advance Billing processes customer data solely as necessary to render the invoicing service.

11 Data Fiduciary & Data Processor Roles

Depending on the context and applicable law:

  • Platform as Data Fiduciary: Advance Billing acts as a Data Fiduciary regarding user account registration data (user names, email addresses, login credentials).
  • Platform as Data Processor: Where users enter customer, vendor, or invoice details into the Platform, the user acts as the Data Fiduciary and Advance Billing acts as a Data Processor carrying out service instructions.

12 Third-Party Infrastructure & Service Providers

To operate a high-availability cloud platform, Advance Billing relies on specialized third-party infrastructure providers who process or store data solely to fulfill infrastructure operations:

  • Application Server Hosting: Render (Render Services Inc.) for web application instance hosting and execution.
  • Database Infrastructure: Supabase (Supabase Inc. / PostgreSQL) for persistent managed relational database storage.
  • Transactional Email Delivery: Brevo (Sendinblue GmbH) operating over secure SMTP (Port 2525) for invoice email dispatches.
  • Media Asset Storage: Cloudinary (Cloudinary Ltd.) for hosting uploaded company logos, signatures, and letterhead assets.
  • Cache & Rate Limiting: Redis managed cache backend for session management and rate limiting.
  • External Cron Triggering: cron-job.org for invoking automated weekly backup schedules via secure authenticated webhook endpoints.
  • Uptime Monitoring: UptimeRobot for monitoring public application health endpoints.

Each of the above infrastructure providers is engaged under terms that require them to implement appropriate technical and organizational safeguards for any data they process on our behalf, and to notify us promptly in the event of a security incident affecting that data. Advance Billing exercises reasonable diligence in selecting and monitoring these providers.

13 Third-Party Security Events & Limitations

Advance Billing takes reasonable measures within its control to protect information processed through the Service, including engaging infrastructure providers under appropriate data protection terms. However, the Service depends on independent third-party infrastructure, and a security incident occurring solely within infrastructure controlled by an independent third party may be outside Advance Billing's direct technical control.

This section limits our liability to users arising from such third-party incidents to the extent permitted by applicable law. It does not limit or exclude any statutory obligation we owe as a Data Fiduciary under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 — including our obligation to exercise reasonable diligence in selecting processors, to respond to a Personal Data Breach as described in Section 13A below, and to remain accountable to the Data Protection Board of India for data we determine the purpose and means of processing. Nothing in this Policy excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

13A Personal Data Breach Notification

In the event Advance Billing becomes aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting personal data we process, we will:

  • Notify the Data Protection Board of India in the manner and timeframe prescribed under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable Rules;
  • Notify affected Data Principals without undue delay, describing the nature of the breach, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address it, to the extent required by law;
  • Take reasonable steps to contain, investigate, and remediate the breach, including coordinating with any third-party infrastructure provider involved.

Where a breach originates at a third-party infrastructure provider, we will act on notification from that provider and coordinate remediation and disclosure steps consistent with our obligations under applicable law.

14 Data Security Safeguards

We implement technical and organizational security controls to protect information:

  • HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption for all data transmitted between web browsers and application servers.
  • Cryptographic password hashing utilizing PBKDF2 / Argon2 algorithms.
  • Multi-tenant database query isolation, restricting organization data strictly to authenticated organization members.
  • In-memory PDF rendering preventing persistent disk caching of temporary invoice files.
  • Socket timeout protections and API rate limiting against brute-force attacks.

Security Notice: While we employ reasonable technical safeguards and incident response procedures, no electronic transmission or cloud storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15 Account Credentials & Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of account passwords and login credentials. Users must notify Advance Billing immediately of any suspected unauthorized access or compromise of their account credentials.

16 Backups & Data Recovery

Advance Billing provides an automated weekly data backup feature that generates structured JSON and Excel exports of organization data and emails them to organization owners.

Automated backups exist for business continuity. Transmitting backup attachments via email involves inherent risks associated with third-party email infrastructure. System backups are not a substitute for the user's independent statutory record-keeping obligations. Users should maintain local offline copies of critical accounting records.

17 Email Communications

Advance Billing sends service-related communications, including password reset links, account notifications, invoice delivery dispatches, and weekly backup files. We do not send unsolicited marketing emails.

18 Data Retention

Account and business data is retained for as long as your organization account remains active. Upon account deletion, active records are purged from production databases within 30 days, subject to temporary persistence in encrypted backup archives for up to 90 days prior to scheduled rotation and permanent deletion.

Technical access logs and error traces are retained for up to 90 days for security and troubleshooting purposes before automated purging. Certain business records (e.g., invoice data) may be retained beyond account closure where required by applicable Indian statutory accounting or tax regulations, for the minimum period mandated by such law.

19 Data Deletion & Lifecycle Purging

Users may initiate organization data deletion via account Danger Zone settings or by submitting a written request to our Grievance Officer. Upon confirmed account deletion:

  • Active production database records are permanently purged within 30 days of confirmed account closure.
  • Encrypted system backup snapshots rotate and overwrite within a 90-day backup rotation lifecycle, after which data is permanently erased.
  • Statutory tax and accounting records may be retained longer strictly as mandated by the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 and applicable rules.

20 Your Privacy Rights (DPDP Act, India)

Subject to identity verification and statutory exemptions under Indian law, Data Principals possess the following rights regarding their digital personal data:

  • Right to Summary & Access: Request a summary of personal data being processed and the identities of data processors.
  • Right to Correction & Completion: Request correction, updating, or completion of inaccurate or misleading personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of personal data where processing is no longer necessary or consent is withdrawn.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: Access readily available grievance redressal mechanisms regarding data processing.
  • Right to Nominate: Nominate an individual to exercise data principal rights in the event of death or incapacity.

We will acknowledge data principal requests within 7 business days and resolve them within 30 days, or as otherwise required by applicable law. To register or update a nominee to exercise rights in the event of death or incapacity, contact our Grievance Officer with the nominee's full name, relationship, and contact details.
