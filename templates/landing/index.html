Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support inventory management?
No. Advance Billing is intentionally designed to be a pure billing platform. By removing complex inventory and accounting modules, we keep the software extremely simple, fast, and easy to use for shop owners and freelancers.
Is my data secure?
Yes. We use industry-standard encryption, secure connections (HTTPS), and isolated database architecture. You can also download a complete backup of your business data at any time.
Can I use my own letterhead?
Yes. You can choose to use our standard professional invoice headers, or upload your pre-printed business letterhead design.
